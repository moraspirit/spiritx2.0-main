# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Primary: undergraduates at Sri Lankan universities, mostly arriving on a phone from a WhatsApp group, Instagram story or Facebook post. They have a few seconds of attention and want to know: what is this, is it for me, what can I build, when is it, how do I register my team.

Secondary: sponsors, judges, mentors and university staff checking that the event is credible — who organises it, how big it is, what happened at Spirit X 1.0.

## Product Purpose
Spirit X 2.0 is the inter-university sports-innovation hackathon organised by the University of Moratuwa and MoraSpirit 360: 48 hours, 200+ hackers, 20+ universities. The site exists to turn a curious student into a registered team. Success means a visitor scrolls the whole story (what it is → tracks → timeline → experience → past stories → register) without thinking the site ended early, and reaches the register action knowing exactly what happens next.

## Brand Personality
Cinematic, athletic, ambitious. The night-stadium world: floodlights, motion, the feeling of walking onto a big stage. The voice is short, direct and energetic ("Reinvent the game."), never corporate. Emotional goal: a student should feel this is the biggest stage they can play on this year, and that their team belongs on it.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS landing pages: icon-card grids, gradient blobs, hero-metric templates.
- Cyberpunk hacker cliché: matrix green, terminal fonts, glitch text, code rain.
- University notice board: dense text, clip-art, flat corporate blue, PDF-poster layouts.
- Positive reference for flow and motion: hackx.lk — one long page, fixed pill nav with a sliding scroll-spy highlight, and a pinned cinematic timeline. Take the structure and motion quality, not its visuals: Spirit X keeps its own Night Stadium theme.

## Design Principles
1. **The story keeps going.** Every section hands off to the next; nothing should look like an ending until the footer.
2. **Stage, not brochure.** Full-bleed media and motion carry the emotion; copy stays short and lets the imagery do the talking.
3. **Always know where you are.** The nav reflects the current chapter, and every pinned sequence shows progress and offers a way out.
4. **Phone first, floodlit.** Designed for a student on a mid-range Android on mobile data: posters before video, no loader screens, motion that stays smooth.
5. **Keep the current theme.** Evolve the existing Night Stadium tokens, logo blue and day/night art pairs; don't introduce a new visual language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both colour schemes. Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative (pinned sequences become static layouts). Save-Data and reduced motion show posters instead of video. Full keyboard navigation with visible focus; touch targets of at least 44px.
